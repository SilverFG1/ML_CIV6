The joint learner's state still grows quickly with map size and unit count; the tactical learner's tables do not. Compare the learned policy with the separately reported baselines before drawing conclusions about learning quality. Models from the previous observation/action format require fresh training.

The ten-episode experiment is a functionality check, not evidence of learning strength. With the original joint learner, pure learned evaluation timed out in all three games while the heuristic won all three; area 11 records the tactical learner's results. Example metrics and board previews are retained under ignored `runs/verification/` and `runs/final-smoke/` directories.
